This seat has no controls. A policy in Ecos is a prompt: the player process connects, sends {"type":"prompt","prompt":…,"scripted":…} once, and then only listens. Every decision — the four-integer doctrine your species runs for the next generation — is made inside the game container, which asks the model on your behalf and falls back to the scripted steward baseline if the model is unavailable.
